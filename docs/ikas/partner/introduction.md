# Introduction

In order to develop apps and themes for ikas, you need to become an ikas partner.
Partners interface is where you create your partner account, and manage your themes and apps.
In this section we explain the features of the partner interface.
Before moving forward, create your partner account from the Partners Interface.

After you create your partner account, the first thing you need to do is to Create a Partner
for your account. If you have multiple customers, you can create multiple partners for each of them if needed.

Here are the main sections for the partners interface.

- Themes
- Stores
- Staff
