# Create App on Partners

Before starting development you need create app on ikas Partners then you can use the generated App Key and App Secret in your app.
To create an app on Partners please follow the steps below:

- Open Apps page
- Click the Create New App button
- Fill the required fields on Create New App page and click the Save Button
- Find your app on apps list and click the row
- Scroll to App Credentials section, and you can copy the App Key and App Secret to your app.
